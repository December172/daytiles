# DayTiles 0.2.0

[简体中文（默认）](0.2.0.md) | [English](0.2.0.en.md) | [繁體中文（香港）](0.2.0.zh_HK.md) | [繁體中文（台灣）](0.2.0.zh_TW.md)

This milestone redesigns the planner around a full-height month calendar, compact navigation, and quick task entry while preserving existing offline data.

## Changes

- Five primary tabs: Schedule, View, Target, Widget, and Me, with shared toolbars and a redesigned side drawer.
- Sunday-first month view with adjacent-month dates, swipe navigation, a year/month picker, offline lunar labels, and task, journal, and sticker previews.
- Flat task lists with circular completion controls and a bottom quick-entry panel for titles, details, tags, priority, and optional reminders.
- Full task editing and subtasks retained, with keyboard-safe toolbars and corrected drawer back handling.
- A weekly date strip and editor for daily/weekly completion targets, a widget catalogue, and grouped local planning tools.
- Updated light/dark appearance, Simplified Chinese UI, and privacy-safe screenshots in the README.

The Room schema is unchanged. Journals, tasks, and stickers remain separate, locally stored models; there is no account or backend requirement.

## Validation

- Offline `assembleDebug`, `testDebugUnitTest`, and `lintDebug` passed: 10 unit tests, zero lint errors, and 50 lint warnings.
- Device checks covered task creation, completion, editing, subtasks, deletion, navigation, month selection, drawer back handling, light/dark appearance, and Simplified Chinese.
- A database comparison confirmed every pre-existing table retained its original rows after device checks; temporary QA data was removed.

## Download and limitations

- `DayTiles-0.2.0-debug.apk` is an installable, debug-signed testing build, matching the distribution approach used for 0.1.0. It is not a Play Store-ready artifact.
- `SHA256SUMS` contains the APK checksum.
- Widget pinning depends on the launcher; successful desktop placement was not confirmed on the test device.
- Lunar labels do not include solar terms or public-holiday annotations.
- The redesign follows the reference app's main appearance; it does not reproduce every reference screen or theme pixel for pixel.
